# Normal Proportions

Normal-distribution word problems. Each question gives a real-world setting with a mean and standard deviation, then asks for the proportion of the data to the left of a value, to the right of it, or between two values. A shaded bell curve shows the region, and a standard normal table with Negative z and Positive z tabs sits beside it for lookups. Choose Mixed, Left, Right or Between, and z to tenths or to hundredths. Feedback names common slips: giving the left area for a right question, forgetting to subtract the smaller entry, or giving the area outside the two values. Hints walk through the z-score, the table cell (highlighted in the table), and the subtraction. First-try score and streak are kept on the page only.

Open `index.html` in a browser, no build step needed.
